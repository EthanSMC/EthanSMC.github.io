# Digital Ethan Identity Card

## Anchors

- Young Chinese man with short, slightly tousled black hair and round thin-frame glasses.
- Calm, analytical expression; composed posture and realistic simplified proportions.
- Clean hand-drawn black-ink outline with restrained skin tone and clothing color.

## Wardrobe

- Off-white crewneck sweater or clean black crewneck sweater.
- Dark navy straight trousers and simple white sneakers.

## Avoid

- Cute mascot proportions, anime features, exaggerated expression, polished corporate-vector rendering.
- Heavy backgrounds or props that hide the face, glasses, hair silhouette, or posture.

## Usage

- Personal website, Chinese article illustrations, product explanations, and creator-system stories.
