# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is readers who encounter Ethan through his writing, product work, or AI-building practice. They should be able to understand who he is, how he thinks, and move naturally from the portfolio narrative into Writing.

## Product Purpose

Present Ethan's fintech product experience, AI-agent building practice, and ongoing writing as one coherent personal body of work. Success means a reader quickly understands Ethan's perspective, trusts the substance behind it, and continues into an article, project, repository, or contact path.

## Brand Personality

Professional, sincere, and creative. The voice should feel thoughtful and grounded rather than promotional, with a recognizably personal hand-drawn identity.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a dark sci-fi portfolio, SaaS dashboard, generic avatar-led personal site, dense hero information wall, or repetitive card-grid template. Avoid decorative polish that competes with the writing or makes professional content feel unserious.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a human point of view, then let experience and artifacts substantiate it.
- Make reading the natural continuation of the personal introduction.
- Preserve the hand-drawn identity without sacrificing clarity or professional credibility.
- Keep navigation and interaction quiet enough that content remains primary.
- Show depth progressively instead of presenting an information wall.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic structure, reduced-motion alternatives, readable contrast, zoom resilience, color-independent meaning, and complete content fallbacks when WebGL or client-side enhancement is unavailable.
